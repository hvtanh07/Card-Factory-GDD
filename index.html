Section 01

Gameplay Overview

Genre Sorting Puzzle / Stack Clear
Theme Ship & Container Logistics
Core Objective Match Goods & Pack Containers

● Goods Stack (Card Factory) is a spatial sorting puzzle game where players untangle stacked layers of colored cargo containers to completely clear the ship's deck.

● By dispatching containers to the parking slots and directing cardboard box items onto conveyor tracks, players match products with corresponding colored containers to complete shipment orders.

● The game emphasizes logical sequence planning—selecting the correct dispatch order of containers prevents parking slot congestion and conveyor jam states.

Core Gameplay Loop

Play Stage Level
Win Level ➔ Earn Coins
Advance to Next Stage
Fail Stage ➔ Revive / Retry

Victory Condition

  • The level is won when ALL CONTAINERS on the board have been fully packed and dispatched.
  • Upon victory, awards player with coin rewards based on performance.

Defeat Condition (Slot Gridlock)

  • Occurs when all open Parking Slots are occupied by incomplete containers.
  • If no further moves can unpack items or free a slot, a Game-Over popup appears.
REVIVE MECHANIC: Reviving automatically triggers the Magnet Booster 3 times, immediately removing 3 box items from the conveyor belt out of the game to relieve congestion!
Section 02

Core Game Mechanics

Part 2.1

Primary Gameplay Mechanics

1 Colored Containers & Multi-Layer Board

State A: Puzzle Board Layout
3D Stack
  • 3D Multi-Layer Layout: Containers are arranged in overlapping 3D geometric configurations (crosses, elevated platforms).
  • Occlusion & Lock System: Unblocked top containers automatically open lids to reveal contents. Blocked containers underneath cannot be tapped.
  • Selection & Transfer (Left-to-Right): Tapping an unblocked container animates it sliding into the FIRST AVAILABLE parking slot from left to right.
State B: Parking Slots & Processing
Staging Area
  • Parking Slot Staging: Buffer line with 4 default parking slots to stage containers awaiting full cargo.
  • Auto-Unpack Mechanism: When container arrives, non-matching cardboard box items fly up onto the conveyor belt track.
  • Item Matching & Completion: Items on belt matching container color fly inside. Reaching capacity seals container and frees parking slot.

2 Conveyor Belt Track (Cargo Buffer)

Stores loose cardboard box items extracted from dispatched containers. The conveyor serves as an active logistics buffer: when containers arrive at parking slots, loose items with matching colors are immediately ingested from the belt. Conveyor will not limit the numbers of item on the belt but will limit the number of containers on the parking slot.

Part 2.2

Secondary & Auxiliary Mechanics

Ads Slot Unlock

Extra Slot

An auxiliary parking slot unlocked by watching a Rewarded Video Ad. Once unlocked, it operates identically to a standard Parking Slot for the rest of the current stage—providing an extra staging buffer to prevent gridlock.

Availability: Always visible next to default slots; unlocked per single stage session.

Speed-Up Feature (2x)

Toggleable

Toggleable 2x game speed mode. Multiplies conveyor belt scroll velocity, item flying trajectories, and container slide animations to accelerate gameplay pacing for experienced players.

State Retention: Persists across stage restarts until player toggles back to 1x.

Spare Wood Container

Self-Clearing

A static wooden crate placed on the puzzle board containing pre-set cargo stacks. Unlike colored containers that occupy parking slots, a Wood Container automatically shatters and disappears as soon as its last item is dispatched.

Board Impact: Instantly frees space underneath without taking any parking slot.
Section 03

Special Puzzle Elements

Special elements introduce sequence challenges and dynamic tactical depth to higher level stages.

🌈

Rainbow Container

Conceals true target color under a rainbow wrapper while blocked. Once fully unblocked, permanently reveals true color and acts as standard container.

🚢

Ship Door Spawner

Fixed ship portal containing queued container stack with counter UI. When exit container is dispatched, counter decrements by -1 and spawns next container.

🧊

Frozen Container

Encased in ice block with countdown value K. While unblocked, dispatching ANY other container on board decrements K by -1. Reaching 0 shatters ice.

🔒

Locked Slot

Parking slot disabled at start with countdown S. Dispatching any container to an open slot decrements S by -1. Reaching 0 removes lock into an empty slot.

Section 04

Boosters & Power-ups

📦⚡

Booster 1: Quick Supply (Extract Target Color Cargo)

Extracts 4 Matching Color Items to Feed Conveyor & Parking Slots
Conveyor Cargo Supply
Target Color Selection Logic:
  • The game randomly selects 1 colored container currently present in the Parking Slots.
  • If there are no colored containers currently in the parking slots, the game selects 1 random color present within the current level.
Cargo Extraction & Conveyor Delivery:

Once the target color is determined, the game automatically pulls out 4 box items of that selected color from containers stacked below on the board and sends them up onto the conveyor belt track. This directly supplies the vital cards/boxes needed to match and clear containers or box items on the conveyor.

🧲

Booster 2: Magnet Pull

Targets Largest Parking Container
Target Biggest Parking Container

● Target Selection Logic: Automatically selects the BIGGEST container currently sitting in the Parking Slots (the container with the largest total item capacity).

Resource Acquisition Hierarchy (Fills R required items):
1. Priority 1 (Conveyor): Extract items directly from conveyor belt track & queue.
2. Priority 2 (Active Board): Extract items from unblocked containers on the board stack.
3. Priority 3 (Deep Fallback): Penetrates Blocked Containers, Rainbow Masks, Spawners & Frozen Ice layers.
🏗️

Booster 3: Crane Grabber & Swap

Board Stack Container Target Only
Board Stack Target Exclusive

● Target Constraint: Allows player to tap to select any unblocked container ONLY FROM THE PUZZLE BOARD STACK (containers in parking slots CANNOT be selected).

● Smart Cardboard Box Swapping: If target container T contains mismatched items of Color D, pulling Color C items from source container S triggers a 1-to-1 swap, transferring Color D items back to source S to conserve total level items.

Section 08

Life & Energy System ❤️ Core Economy

System Overview & Design Goals

Session Pacing, Loss Prevention & Monetization Funnel

● The Life System is a pacing engine that limits the number of consecutive failed attempts a player can make before pausing or waiting for a refill.

● Players are encouraged to play thoughtfully to preserve their lives, while non-paying players can wait for natural auto-refills or engage with rewarded monetization systems to keep playing immediately.

Max & Refill Logic

Auto-Timer
  • Maximum Capacity: Each player starts with a maximum cap of 5 lives.
  • Auto-Refill Trigger: Whenever current lives < 5, an internal countdown timer initiates immediately.
  • 20-Minute Pace: Each single life takes exactly 20 minutes (1,200 seconds) to refill.
  • Stop Condition: Once total lives reach 5/5 again, the auto-refill countdown timer stops and HUD shows "FULL".
// Offline Refill Formula:
Elapsed = CurrentTimestamp - LastRefillTimestamp
Gained = floor(Elapsed / 1200)
Lives = min(5, CurrentLives + Gained)

Life Deplete Logic

-1 Per Fail/Restart

A player will lose exactly 1 life (-1) in the following two explicit trigger cases:

Case A: Lose Level & Restart

When all parking slots are blocked (Gridlock) and player either declines the revive option or exhausts revives and taps "Retry" or "Exit".

Case B: Manual Level Restart

When the player pauses the game or opens in-game settings and explicitly confirms "Restart Level" before completing the stage.

Safe Actions (0 Life Deducted):
• Winning a level | • Entering a level initially | • Successfully reviving with booster

Feature Mechanics & Progressive Ladder Rules

  • 1. Daily Free Instant Gift (No Ads): Available once every 24-hour cycle. Clicking 'Collect' immediately awards 150 coins without showing any advertisement.
  • 2. 4-Step Progressive Ad Ladder: Players can watch up to 4 Rewarded Video Ads sequentially. Rewards increase per step: 150 ➔ 200 ➔ 250 ➔ 300 Coins (Total: 900 Coins from ads).
  • 3. Strict Sequential Unlocking: Tier N+1 remains locked until Tier N is successfully watched and claimed. Players cannot skip ahead to higher coin tiers.
  • 4. Daily Cycle Reset (Renew in hh:mm:ss): The entire ladder and free claim reset simultaneously at 00:00 UTC (or every 24h). Unclaimed steps do not roll over.
